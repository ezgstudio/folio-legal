# Folio

Marketing site and legal pages for the **Folio** iOS app by EZG Studio LLC.

Hosted via GitHub Pages at [https://ezgstudio.github.io/folio-legal/](https://ezgstudio.github.io/folio-legal/).

- [Home / landing page](index.html)
- [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)
- [Terms of Service](terms.html)

## Notes

- `index.html` is the marketing landing page (self-contained; assets live in `assets/`).
- `privacy.html` and `terms.html` are linked from App Store Connect and inside the app —
  **do not change their filenames or URLs.** They share `style.css`.
- At App Store launch, replace the `#get` placeholder links in `index.html` with the real
  App Store URL (two "Coming soon to the App Store" badges + the nav "Get Folio" button).
